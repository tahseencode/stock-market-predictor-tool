Mathematical Reality & Risk Disclosure

Under the Efficient Market Hypothesis and Random Walk Theory, future stock price movements are stochastic processes influenced by unforeseen catalysts (earnings surprises, central bank policy shifts, geopolitical headlines, and order flow). No algorithm can achieve 100% certainty. Quantitative trading systems focus on statistical expected value, out-of-sample directional edges (55%–65% win rates), and strict asymmetric risk management.

QuantPulse AI is an algorithmic research and educational platform. It does not provide financial or trading advice.
